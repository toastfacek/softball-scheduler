# Golf Site Design Audit — BGSL Tournament Direction

## References

- Masters: tradition, restraint, deep green, ivory, minimal CTAs, ceremonial pacing.
- PGA Tour: tournament utility, scoreboards, stats strips, clear information hierarchy.
- LIV Golf: editorial sports energy, large media surfaces, bold modular promotion.

## Direction

Position the BGSL page as a premium Beverly golf outing, not a software product page.

Visual identity:

- Deep evergreen masthead with restrained antique-gold accents
- Ivory paper sections with quiet club-program typography
- Serif tournament headlines paired with compact uppercase navigation and labels
- Scoreboard-style event strip for date, time, venue, and format
- Sponsor cards that feel like premium outing packages, not SaaS pricing tiers
- BGSL texture used as atmosphere only; replace with real course/candid photos when available

What to avoid:

- Rounded SaaS cards, gradient blobs, oversized app-style hero metrics
- Fake photo labels or abstract golf graphics pretending to be photography
- Overuse of BGSL logo or orange as the dominant brand signal
- Generic pricing-page composition
